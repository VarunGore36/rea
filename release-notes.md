## [6.6.0](https://github.com/VarunGore36/rea/compare/rea-agents-6.5.0...rea-agents-6.6.0) (2026-10-14)


###   BREAKING CHANGES

* **managed:** unresolved managed/native verification results may retain a non-null matched_native and candidate facts when module identity is unknown. Consumers must use status and reason rather than assume unresolved observations are empty.
* **adb:** install_adb_package now requires replace_requested and exposes replaced as nullable rather than claiming the selected mode was an observed replacement.
* **identity:** server.command_path may be null when the invocation path is unavailable. Registration and setup statuses, and doctor classifications, may report unknown.
* **providers:** binary_overview segment length is null when Hopper coordinates do not establish a finite same-address-space extent. Consumers must preserve this unknown instead of treating it as zero or a saturated value.
* **providers:** MIPS targets with unresolved GNU ABI attributes and saved Hopper databases on macOS are refused rather than advertised as supported.
* **cli:** keep a selected JSON null in --filter-output output ([#2333](https://github.com/VarunGore36/rea/issues/2333))
